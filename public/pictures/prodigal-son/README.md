# The Prodigal Son production package

The screenplay adapts Luke 15:11–32 around both sons and the father who goes out to meet each of them.

## Contents

- Prodigal_Son_Complete_Screenplay.docx: complete screenplay, performance notes, historical decisions and linked sources.
- Prodigal_Son.fountain: editable plain-text screenplay with stable scene IDs and timing notes.
- Prodigal_Son_Visual_Asset_Inventory.xlsx: filterable assets, scene timings, normalized scene associations and continuity requirements.
- assets.json, scene_asset_links.json, scene_metadata.json, continuity.json and inventory_data.json: structured versions of the inventory data.
- Research_and_Adaptation_Notes.md: research findings and adaptation boundaries.

## Runtime

22 narrative scenes including the opening title total 29 minutes 30 seconds. End credits add 30 seconds. The 30-minute timing plan is a production target, to be confirmed through performance and an animatic.

## Use

Read the screenplay before developing images. Each inventory record identifies an asset or reference package, with any required states and scene uses. A record is not a generated image count. Complete visual development and approve the canonical face, costume, location and object references before cinematography and prompt development. Prompts must identify the approved asset and version. First and last frames require approval before video generation.

## Story boundaries

The father visibly loves both sons. The younger confesses his sin after his father has embraced him. The older brother’s response to the invitation remains unresolved. Documents, gifts, precise geography and private scenes are dramatic inventions; they are not presented as events recorded by Luke.
