# H02 — Revised Spoken Dialogue

Same speakers, same beats, same theology. Lines are lengthened so TTS and the scene have context. `Be still.` stays short on purpose.

## Transcript

**TORTURER**  
Renounce the promise, first father, and this ends. One word from you, and I loose the hook.

**ADAM**  
The promise is not mine to renounce. I did not speak it into being, and I will not unsay it here.

**TORTURER**  
You are dust. Dust has no inheritance. Tell me what ashes may claim from the living God.

**ADAM**  
I am dust—and guilty before Him. Yet the dust is His, and still I belong to God.

**TORTURER (O.S.)**  
Look upon the inheritance of rebellion. Kingdoms fall, names perish, and every proud crown is broken here.

**EVE**  
Adam—look at me. Let not his darkness make strangers of us again.

**MOSES**  
Hold fast, Adam. These chains may bind the hand, but not the promise. You do not wait alone.

**MOSES**  
He claims no innocence, and asks no wage. He waits upon the mercy of the God who judges him.

**TORTURER**  
Your Messiah is dead. His blood is cold, and the grave has sealed its mouth upon Him.

**MOSES**  
Then why do you tremble? You did not gather the faithful by judgment. You seized them because His death frightened you.

**TORTURER**  
Then let Him come. Let Him descend into my kingdom. Adam shall be the pledge of His defeat.

**TORTURER**  
You blamed her once, when fear found you in the garden. Will you condemn her again, here before all the dead?

**EVE**  
No. I chose the serpent’s lie, and the wound is mine. I will not escape my guilt by choosing your lie now.

**TORTURER**  
In the garden, when judgment found you, you set her name between yourself and the face of God.

**ADAM**  
I named her when I should have confessed. I made of her a shield for my fear. Never again.

**ADAM**  
He found us hiding among the trees, clothed only in shame. Yet He covered us with His own hand.

**ADAM**  
Then He promised the woman’s Son would crush the serpent. The wound would be His, and the victory would be God’s.

**TORTURER**  
Sons beyond counting have crossed these gates: kings, prophets, beggars, all swallowed by the same night.

**TORTURER**  
So where is the Son you were promised? Why does He not answer from beyond the stone?

**DAVID**  
You will not abandon my soul to Sheol. Even in the depths, I will wait for You.

**DAVID**  
Nor will You let Your Holy One see corruption. The grave shall not possess Him.

**JOHN**  
Be still.

**JOHN**  
I know that tread. I heard it beside the Jordan, when heaven opened and the waters bore witness.

**JOHN**  
Death has taken the One whom death cannot hold. The grave has opened its mouth upon its own undoing.
