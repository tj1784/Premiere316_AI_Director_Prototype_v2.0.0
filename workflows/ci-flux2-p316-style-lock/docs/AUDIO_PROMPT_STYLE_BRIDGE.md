# Audio Prompt Style Bridge — Premiere316

Use this for Voices, Sound Design, and Music rows. It translates the visual design into audio language while keeping the row prompt as the content source.

## Shared audio style
Sacred cinematic realism, ancient stone space, warm gold light translated as warm harmonic glow, dark smoky shadows translated as low-frequency pressure and restrained reverb, ember haze translated as fine crackle and drifting air, live-action grit translated as close-mic detail and natural dynamic range.

## Voice direction
Grounded, reverent, emotionally intense but not theatrical. Breath is present but controlled. Delivery feels live-action and human, with chamber/cavern resonance only when the scene location calls for it. No synthetic announcer polish.

## Sound design direction
Prioritize believable physical sources: cloth movement, bare feet or sandals only when the row calls for them, stone scrape, metallic ring, distant crowd bed, low cavern air, ember crackle, ash drift, sword/holy-energy hum only when requested. Avoid random monster sounds unless the row requests them.

## Music direction
Sacred epic score with restraint: low choir, bowed bass, bronze/copper resonance, ancient frame drum, sparse strings, slow harmonic lift, silence and breath between phrases. Avoid modern trailer drums unless the row asks for aggressive battle scoring.
