# Category Prompt Envelopes — Same Artistic Design Without Copying Content

Paste the row's existing generation prompt under the matching envelope when a row needs reinforcement. Keep the row's original content as source-of-truth.

## Characters — 11 rows
Create the named character from the row prompt only. Preserve the row's age, ethnicity, role, wardrobe, wounds, props, and continuity locks. Use the style references only for cinematic lighting, palette, realism, texture, and lens language. Do not copy the Jesus face, hair, beard, robe, sword, wounds, or body unless that exact row says so.

## Wardrobe — 12 rows
Generate the garment/costume asset described by the row. Use the style references for fabric realism, linen weave, practical distressing, warm/cool shadow contrast, and film-production material quality. Do not turn unrelated wardrobe into white burial cloth unless requested.

## Locations — 14 rows
Generate the location described by the row. Use the references for sacred scale, smoky atmosphere, light beams, ember haze, wet/dry stone texture, and cinematic depth. Do not copy the hell gate or cave layout unless requested.

## Props and Artifacts — 10 rows
Generate the prop/artifact described by the row. Use the references for metal/stone/wood/cloth material realism, golden rim highlights, engraved detail, and practical aging. Do not add a glowing sword unless requested.

## Crowds and Extras — 5 rows
Generate the crowd/extras described by the row. Use the references for dramatic staging, chiaroscuro, haze, and motion energy. Do not copy demons or the same pleading hands unless requested.

## Atmosphere and VFX — 13 rows
Generate the atmosphere/VFX layer described by the row. Use the references for warm gold versus smoky black/brown color design, ember particles, volumetric light, dust, ash, and sacred glow. Keep it clean enough for compositing.

## Guide Frames — 2 rows
Use the 16X9 workflow. Generate the exact camera framing and story beat from the row prompt. References control only the color grade, cinematic exposure, atmosphere, texture, and production-value feel.

## Voices — 8 rows
Do not use the image workflow. Use `AUDIO_PROMPT_STYLE_BRIDGE.md` to convert the visual style into vocal direction: sacred, grounded, breath, reverence, grit, chamber resonance, and emotional intensity.

## Sound Design — 15 rows
Do not use the image workflow. Use the audio bridge for Foley/ambient language: stone resonance, linen motion, ember crackle, air pressure, sword hum, crowd distance, cavern slapback, and restrained sacred power.

## Music — 6 rows
Do not use the image workflow. Use the audio bridge for score direction: sacred low choir, ancient percussion, bowed bass, bronze resonance, slow triumphant harmonic lift, and restrained epic scale.

## Graphics — 1 row
For non-text graphics, use the image workflow. For text-bearing graphics, generate the background/texture plate here and do final typography in an editor to avoid AI text artifacts.
